The Philosophy

Precision without
complexity.

The Loftin Method runs a calculated deficit tuned to your exact body, phase, and lifestyle. It knows when you're drinking. It adjusts for restaurants and travel. It does the thinking — you do the work.

01
Calorie math built for your body
Your daily target is calculated from your weight, height, age, and current phase — not a generic 1,500-calorie template. It recalculates automatically as you progress through five deficit phases.
02
Movement as a lever, not a punishment
Daily step targets are calibrated to your exact burn goal. Normal days target 500 calories walking. Drinking days step up to 750. No gym membership required.
03
Real food. No substitutes.
Chicken, steak, salmon — portioned precisely for your calorie target and weighed in grams. No protein shakes, no meal bars, no supplements. Food you'd actually want to eat.
04
Alcohol is built into the math
Not banned. Not ignored. Every drinking night has a calorie reserve. Meals adjust down. Steps adjust up. You can have a social life and still lose weight — that's the whole point.

Four steps. No app store. Works on any iPhone, right now.

02

Exact meals. Exact weights in grams. Exact step target. A complete day built for your body, updated every morning.
